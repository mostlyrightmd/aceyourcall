Data on this instance

The operator controls this instance and its OpenAI account. Application context is temporarily processed in memory and sent to OpenAI; optional browser drafts stay on this device. Transcripts and scorecards are stored for up to 30 days and may contain application facts. Public interviewer research is cached. No raw audio or video is recorded by this app. Ask the operator about access, deletion, backups, provider policies and any enabled analytics before sharing information.
